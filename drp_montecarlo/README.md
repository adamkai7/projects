# Monte Carlo Projects: UC Davis Department of Mathematics Directed Reading Program

I am an undergraduate at UC Davis, majoring in Mathematical and Scientific Computation. This repo holds my work for the UC Davis Department of Mathematics Directed Reading Program (DRP), where I'm exploring Monte Carlo methods through a series of readings and projects.

## About the DRP

The Directed Reading Program pairs undergraduates with a mentor for a quarter-long or multi-quarter independent study in a topic not covered in the standard curriculum. We meet regularly to go over the reading and the work, and finish with a presentation.

- **Mentor:** John Walker, PhD Student
- **Topic:** Monte Carlo methods

## What is Monte Carlo?

Monte Carlo methods use repeated random sampling to estimate quantities that are hard or impossible to compute exactly. Each project here picks a problem, simulates it with random draws, and checks how the estimate behaves as the number of samples grows (error typically shrinks like 1/sqrt(N)).
